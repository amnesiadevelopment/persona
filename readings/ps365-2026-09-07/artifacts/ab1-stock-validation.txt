READING — this is the NEGATIVE CONTROL for A/B #1, not a persona measurement.

  Every row is STOCK Chromium. The A/B predicts the engine returns FALSE for
  fractional_dev and metrics_f32; stock must therefore return TRUE for them
  wherever the A/B claims to be valid, or the instrument cannot discriminate.

  1. At every VALID=True scale (0.5, 1, 2, 4) stock returns
     fractional_dev=True and metrics_f32=True — the predicted-stock answer.
     The A/B can therefore distinguish engine from stock at those scales.

  2. VALID correctly reads False at 1.25 / 1.5 / 1.75, which is the guard the
     code review asked for. At those scales fractional_dev=False on STOCK —
     i.e. without the guard the A/B would have returned the "engine detected"
     answer on an unmodified browser.

  3. integral_dev is True on 6 of 7 rows including two invalid ones, which is
     why the report tells the owner to read the fractional_ row. The first
     draft of this A/B used integral geometry ALONE and would have answered
     True at 1.25 and 1.5 — a confirming reading on hardware that proves
     nothing. That is the defect the review caught, reproduced here.

  4. frac_css is False at dpr 2 and 4 while frac_dev is True — the CSS-space
     form of the invariant fails on STOCK at power-of-two scales above 1. This
     is the direct measurement showing the committed CSS-space formulation had
     to be replaced rather than merely annotated.

  5. metrics_f32 is True on ALL SEVEN rows, invalid scales included: invariant
     2 carries no dpr precondition. Confirms inv2-scale-sweep.txt.
