READING
  INVARIANT 2 is 0/1008 at EVERY scale, including the ones where invariant 1
  collapses in the thousands. The asymmetry is structural, not luck:

    invariant 1 is a lattice claim about a value Blink DIVIDED by the scale
    factor, so the divisor decides whether the float32 store is lossless.
    invariant 2 is a REPRESENTATION claim about a value that never passes
    through the scale factor at all — canvas text metrics are computed in
    float and widened at the bindings layer regardless of device scale.

  A representation invariant survives a transform that a lattice invariant
  cannot, because "is this double a float32" is closed under nothing the
  scale factor does to it. That is why invariant 2 needs no dpr precondition
  and invariant 1 does.
