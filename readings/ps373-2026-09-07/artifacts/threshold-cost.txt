== the separation, and what threshold it admits ==
   population A tops out at 16 distinct colours (feder's own ceiling).
   population B starts, on the WEAKEST antialiasing modelled here
   (1 colour pair, 8 coverage steps), at 9 and rises steeply.

   ⚠️ THE POPULATIONS TOUCH at the weak end: 8-step AA on a single
   colour pair yields 9, which is BELOW feder's 16. So a threshold of
   16 is NOT free — it would exempt a hypothetical canvas drawn with a
   single glyph in one colour at coarse AA. Whether such a canvas is a
   real fingerprinting surface is a question for the host, not for me.
   A conservative threshold (i.e. materially lower than 16) buys the
   probe exemption with a strictly smaller exposed population.
